Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what BEPAL stores and why. Please read it before you use the app for your business.

What we store

  • Your account email address.
  • The business details you enter: business name, owner name, phone number, category and currency.
  • Your products, prices, stock, sales, and any customer credit (udhar) records you enter.

Where it is stored

Your account and business data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Your PIN is stored only on the phone where you set it, not in the cloud.

Who can see your data

Each business signs in with its own email and password, and the app is set up so that a business can read only its own data. The people who run BEPAL can technically reach the database to keep the service working, and will use that access only to run and fix BEPAL.

Visit statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits to this website and the BEPAL app, and to see which pages are used. It records things like the pages viewed, device type and approximate location. We do not use it to identify you by name, and we do not put your business data into it.

What we do not do

  • BEPAL does not show advertising.
  • BEPAL does not take payments from your customers.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings. You can download your sales as a spreadsheet, delete your sales history, or delete your account and all its data. You can also write to us and we will help.

Your responsibility

Keep your password and PIN private. Use a real email address that you can open, because it is needed to reset a forgotten password. The credit records you enter about your customers are your responsibility.
