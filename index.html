Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No app store is needed. Open the link and tap Install to add BEPAL to your home screen.

Is my data private?

Each business signs in with its own email and password and can only see its own data.

Can I fix a mistake in a bill?

Yes. Cancel the sale and the items go back into stock.

What if the internet drops?

BEPAL is built to keep working and sync later. Please test it in your shop first.
